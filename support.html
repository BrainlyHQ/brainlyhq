Economy & Drop Rates

What are the exact chances for Mythic & Diamond Chests?

Mythic Chests offer 20 Coins (28%), 60 Coins (25%), 210 Coins (12%), and up to 890 Coins (3%). Diamond Chests feature top-tier multipliers up to 965 Coins!

Pets System

How do Pets work and what abilities do they grant?

Pets like Ollie (1.4x Coin Collector), Kaya (5% discount), Mi Fuego (Profit Burn 2x), and Sally (1.3x XP) provide permanent passive boosts.

Server Regulations

Is Real Money Trading (RMT) or Alt-Account farming allowed?

Strictly forbidden. Violations result in an immediate global economy reset, permanent ban from BrainlyHQ, and blacklisting.

HQ Premium

What benefits do I get with HQ Premium subscription?

Includes 1.5x XP Boost, 10% shop discounts, +5 Coins/24h, exclusive Science Scavengers access, and pre-release feature trials.
